## Audit Selection Rule

Audits were limited to exact locked third-party versions from the TRJ4-160 target families where local evidence was strong enough for this follow-up:

- The crate version is present in the locked Cargo graph.
- The existing exemption required `safe-to-deploy`.
- Local registry metadata was available.
- The manifest declares no build script.
- A local source scan found no unsafe tokens.

This deliberately leaves the larger networking, generated AWS service client, and unsafe-containing crates for later review.
