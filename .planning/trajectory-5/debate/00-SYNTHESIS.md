Six position papers were generated by independent agents on 2026-05-07. Each
defended a distinct thesis with codebase citations. The papers are at
`.planning/trajectory-5/debate/01..06`. This document reconciles them.

## Where the agents converged

Five of six positions independently land on the same diagnosis: trj4's
"structural framing without runtime wiring" pattern (per the
`TRAJECTORY-4-CLOSEOUT-ERRATUM.md`) is not a one-off process failure -- it is
the steady-state symptom of a substrate that is shipping faster than it is
being proven.

The convergent evidence:

- **Mutation kill 31%** workspace banner; trj4 floor is 65% / 80% on
  `chio-attest-verify` (Hawk #1, Quality #1, Productization concession).
- **All 21 `audits/evidence/threats/*.json`** files have `caught: 0`,
  `needs_real_run: true`, `ran_at: "1970-01-01T00:00:00Z"`. The 20/0/0 PASS
  banner is a placeholder. (Quality #1, Hawk #2.)
- **Three deferred Kani crates** (`chio-attest-verify`, `chio-anchor`,
  `chio-weights`) have zero `kani::` references. TRJ4-012/013/014 open.
  (Quality #2.)
- **Hot-path single-entry verifier missing**:
  `verify_capability_full_without_budget_admit` and the legacy
  `verify_capability_signature` are still callable from
  `crates/chio-kernel/src/kernel/mod.rs:4005` and `:4035-4047`, defeating the
  T1.0 capability-negotiation Evidence Gate. (Protocol #1, Hawk #3.)
- **Receipt v2 silently downgrades** to v1 with a warning at
  `chio-kernel/src/kernel/mod.rs:1148-1165` even when negotiation indicated
  `chio.capability.v2`. PROTOCOL.md section 6 lines 714-741 says "fails
  closed" but the runtime says "warns and continues". (Protocol #2.)
- **Anchor-batch sync path** still callable when `require_public_witness=true`
  contradicts PROTOCOL.md §982-991. (Protocol #3.)
- **Architecture forcing the gap**: `chio-kernel::dispatch_tool_call_with_cost`
  is `async` but immediately delegates to a sync helper because the
  `ToolServer` trait is sync-only; 36 `&mut self` setters in the same 6,757-LOC
  file. Without async-trait surgery, hot-path wiring of new primitives keeps
  bouncing off the kernel's existing signature. (Decomposition #1.)
- **Lean4 `negotiation_safety` proven by `rfl`** against its own definition --
  tautology, not refinement. `formal/theorem-inventory.json`: 0 of 75 IDs
  `proven`. (Quality #3.)

These are not seven independent problems. They are one problem in seven
disguises: **claims are made before the call sites are wired and the
fixtures are real.**

## Where the agents disagreed

The Productization Champion (5) and Vision Strategist (6) both argued, with
real force, that hardening without users is hardening that no one stress-tests.
Both conceded a non-negotiable hardening floor before they make their pitch.

The Architecture Decomposition Advocate (3) made the cleanest case for
"refactor first, wire second" but conceded that mounting a separate
decomposition trajectory in parallel with an in-flight 16-wave closeout would
double-fragment attention.

The Substrate Hardening Hawk (1) and Protocol Realization Engineer (2)
explicitly noted their work composes -- realization (R-lanes) and hardening
(W-lanes) want the same Evidence Gate discipline.

The Quality Skeptic (4) is the most damning: even the artifacts that
provenance-cite "Validation" in `TRAJECTORY-4-FINAL.md` are placeholders when
inspected.

## What release work should be

**Trajectory 5 is the honesty trajectory.** Three coupled lanes, no separate
brand from the trj4 wave plan, one ship-bar that is visible from outside.

### Lane A: Realize the floor (~8 weeks, parallelizable)

Absorbs trj4 Wave 0 / Wave 1 / Wave 4 with real evidence requirements.

- Mutation kill: 31% -> >=65% trust-boundary crates, >=80% on
  `chio-attest-verify`. README banner reflects observed kill rate, not target.
- All 21 `audits/evidence/threats/*.json` files contain real `caught >= 1`
  data with non-1970 `ran_at`. Replace placeholder fixture with the production
  call path executed under each threat row.
- Real Kani harnesses for `chio-attest-verify`, `chio-anchor`, `chio-weights`
  (TRJ4-012/013/014).
- TLA+ rewrites: `ReceiptBeforeAllow` split (TRJ4-016), `RevocationCutCompleteness`
  bounded transitive-closure unrolling (TRJ4-015), apalache-temporal lane
  promoted required (TRJ4-018), `EpochMax` 4 -> 6 (TRJ4-017).
- Lean4 `negotiation_safety` re-proved against the executable model, not by
  `rfl` against its own definition.

### Lane B: Wire the spec hot path (~6 weeks)

Adopts Protocol Realization Engineer's R1-R3, plus the architectural
prerequisite from the Decomposition advocate.

- **Architectural prerequisite**: convert `ToolServer` trait to `async_trait`,
  collapse the dispatch sync-helper hop in
  `chio-kernel/src/kernel/mod.rs:6402`. This is the smallest decomposition
  cut that unblocks hot-path wiring; everything else (chio-cli trust-control
  extraction, gravity-well surgery) stays out of release work.
- **Single-entry verifier**: `verify_capability_full` becomes the only
  production path. Delete `verify_capability_full_without_budget_admit`;
  legacy `verify_capability_signature` callers migrate. PROTOCOL.md §408-418
  changes SHOULD -> MUST.
- **Receipt v2 fail-closed under negotiated v2**: replace the warn-and-downgrade
  at `chio-kernel/src/kernel/mod.rs:1148-1165` with a hard reject. PROTOCOL.md
  §714-741 changes "falls back" -> "fails closed".
- **Anchor-batch async-only when public witness required**: gate
  `crates/chio-anchor/src/batch.rs:208-258` sync path; add
  `scripts/check-anchor-batch-async-witness.sh` and a signed negative
  conformance fixture under `chio-conformance`.
- **Each primitive closes with**: enforced call site + spec MUST citation +
  signed negative conformance test that fails when wiring is removed. No
  Evidence Gate row closes without all three.

### Lane C: One forcing demo (~4 weeks)

Adopts the Vision Strategist's chiodome slice and the Productization
Champion's KB-MCP dogfood. One demo -- not two, not five.

- **Two-kernel cross-org bilateral cosigned invocation** using existing
  `crates/chio-federation/src/bilateral.rs` (`CoSigningBody`,
  `DualSignedReceipt`). Per `spec/CHIODOS_BILATERAL_COSIGN_INVOCATION.md` §6.
- **Capability lease + budget bond** via `chio-credit` `CREDIT_BOND_ARTIFACT_SCHEMA`.
- **Anchored** through `crates/chio-anchor::Web3CheckpointStatement` (no new
  Web3 live deployment required; bounded claim).
- **Selective-disclosure auditor view** behind `bbs-stub` Cargo feature flag. No
  new spec ratification. Per `spec/CHIODOS_SELECTIVE_DISCLOSURE.md` §6.
- **Wrapped at the user surface** by `chio mcp serve --policy` against the
  local KB MCP stack at `ops/knowledge-base/`. The receipts produced by the
  bilateral invocation are dogfooded through `chio receipt explain`.
- **Output**: a `v0.1.0-bounded-chiodome` honest release tag under v3.18
  bounded-claim discipline.

## Ship bar (visible from outside)

Trj5 closes when **all three** are observably true:

1. README mutation banner reads >=65% with the per-crate breakdown attached
   and a non-placeholder evidence directory.
2. The three Lane B primitives (capability v2, receipt v2, anchor-batch async)
   are each protected by a signed negative conformance fixture in
   `crates/chio-conformance/tests/` that exercises the production call site
   and fails when the enforcement is removed.
3. The Lane C bilateral demo runs end-to-end, the receipts are inspectable
   with `chio receipt explain`, and the demo run is captured as a fixture
   in `examples/`.

If any of the three slips, release work stays open. No closeout erratum is needed
because the bar is the kind a third party can verify.

## Why this shape

- **It honors the Hawk and the Quality Skeptic**: the floor work is the
  primary lane, and it doesn't paper over the placeholder evidence.
- **It honors the Protocol Realization Engineer**: hot-path wiring runs in
  parallel and uses the same Evidence Gate discipline.
- **It honors the Decomposition Advocate without taking the whole bait**:
  the smallest architectural cut that unblocks Lane B is in scope; the rest
  waits for trj6.
- **It honors the Productization Champion and Vision Strategist**: the
  forcing demo is the customer Chio doesn't have, and it forces the substrate
  to actually compose end-to-end. If Lane C breaks, Lanes A and B aren't
  real either.
- **It rejects** seven-lane menus, parallel new milestones, and any framing
  that lets trj4's pattern repeat.

The honest framing for the project memory and `RELEASE_AUDIT.md`: Chio's
differentiator is the proof artifact. Until the proof artifact is real, every
trajectory after trj4 is the same trajectory wearing a different name.
