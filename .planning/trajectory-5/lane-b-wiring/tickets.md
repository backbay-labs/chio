# Lane B Tickets

Tickets are numbered `release work-B<sublane>.<n>`. Effort is S/M/L (small ~1-2 days, medium ~3-5 days, large >5 days). Acceptance includes the Evidence Gate close bar from `README.md` (enforced call site + spec MUST citation + signed negative conformance test).

**Ticket-ID convention (R1 MAJOR fix)**: per `templates/TICKET-TEMPLATE.md` §38, the canonical Evidence-Gate ticket suffix is `.E` per sub-lane (so `release work-B1.E`, `release work-B2.E`, `release work-B3.E`, `bilateral DSSE signing item`). The previous shorthand `release work-B-EG` and `release work-B.CLOSE` are retired. Every Lane B sub-lane carries one `.E` ticket as its closing instrument; the parser at `scripts/check-release work-evidence-gate.sh` resolves `### release work-X.y` audit-doc blocks against this suffix.
