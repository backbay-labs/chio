# Scholar Position

## Headline argument

A single paper at NDSS or USENIX does not establish a research area; it establishes a citation. Programmable sovereignty over capability-bounded federated receipts is large enough to anchor 3-5 papers over 24 months across security, programming languages, AI safety, and law - and the framework will not define a community unless it is cited internally across a sequence that other researchers can locate. The pragmatist who ships once and stops produces a curiosity. The substrate-builder who polishes Rust before publishing produces a tool no one cites. The scholar's program produces a sub-field. Two of the four v0 drafts are already close to defensible (sensor-grounded ready now; agentic-tool-safety three weeks from workshop-ready); two more become tractable with the right co-author (delegated-emergency-authority needs Walch or equivalent; bounded-executive-action needs the rfl gate cleared). The cost of pipeline-spread is real but bounded: stagger submissions, cap active threads at two, and accept that some v0 drafts will land as workshop papers rather than top-tier conferences.

## The 24-month research-program arc

The publishable area is **constitutional substrate for capability-bounded autonomous systems**: a sequence in which Lean-attestable predicates, federated receipt graphs, and sensor-grounded admission compose into a framework that the security, PL, and AI-safety communities can adopt piece-by-piece.

**2026 Q3-Q4 - Anchor submission.** Parent paper (programmable sovereignty) to NDSS 2027 Fall cycle or USENIX Security 2027 Cycle 1. This is the area-defining citation. Every subsequent paper cites it as foundational; the framework hangs off this one acceptance.

**2026 Q3 - Companion submission (parallel, not sequential).** Sensor-grounded admission to USENIX Security 2027 Cycle 1 (deadline 2026-08-25). Already at 18 pages article-class with mechanized Lean, 32-entry bib, zero substantive findings from final adversarial review. This is the single highest-confidence next ship. It cites the parent's predicate language, extends it with attested substrate state, and proves the parent's claim that the framework supports composition.

**2027 Q1-Q2 - Workshop tier.** Agentic-tool-safety to NeurIPS Safe-AI workshop or ICML AI Safety workshop. Position paper, two-to-three weeks of work, workshop-publishable without co-author. Recasts tool calls as reversible-action admission - reuses the parent substrate, brings the framework into the AI-safety conversation where citations compound fastest. Workshop tier is the right venue: it surfaces the connection without competing for top-conference page budget against the bounded-executive-action paper.

Cites parent and sensor-grounded.

**2027 Q3-Q4 - Delegated emergency authority.** Cross-disciplinary, law-journal target. Requires co-author. 12-18 month timeline from co-author commitment, which means the recruiting has to happen Q3 2026 for a Q4 2027 submission to Yale JOLT or Harvard JOLT.

This is the paper that proves the area is bigger than security: it demonstrates the framework engages legal-philosophy primary sources, not just borrows their vocabulary. Yale/Harvard JOLT Q1 2028.

**2028 Q3 (optional) - Paper 4 trajectory-invariant.** POPL 2029 submission window. Lean-heavy, programming-languages venue. Citation pattern flips: instead of citing parent as foundation, it cites the parent's V5 trajectory-invariant module as the seed and elaborates it into a full PL contribution.

## Risk profile

The dominant risk is **solo-author capacity collapse**. Five active paper threads with one author is canonically how programs fail - none land because attention fragments. Mitigation: at any given moment only two papers are in active drafting. The pipeline staggers: parent + sensor-grounded ship Q3 2026 (both already near-final). Workshop paper drafts Q1 2027 while reversible-action sits behind the rfl gate. Reversible-action drafts Q2-Q3 2027 while emergency-authority is in co-author negotiation. Emergency-authority drafts Q4 2027 - Q3 2028 while Hart-conditions is in research phase.

The secondary risk is **co-author non-response**. Walch may not respond to the pre-disclosure letter; Perez may not commit. Mitigation: the program ships even if every co-author declines. Sensor-grounded is solo, reversible-action is solo, agentic-tool-safety workshop is solo. The co-author-essential papers (emergency-authority, Hart-conditions) get downgraded to long-form-essay venues (Lawfare, Just Security, SSRN working papers) if law-academy partnership fails entirely - still citable, still build the program, lower prestige.

The tertiary risk is **the parent paper failing peer review**. Five polish passes plus eight swarm iterations plus four post-execution reviews is unusual due diligence, but USENIX/NDSS reject 80%+ of submissions. Mitigation: simultaneous-submission policy check was completed; the paper has both NDSS and USENIX targeted. If both reject, IEEE S&P 2027 is the third shot. Three top venues over 12 months is realistic.

## Anticipated counter-arguments

**Pragmatist: "Ship one, see if it lands, then decide."** The pragmatist is right that overcommitting to a pipeline before the anchor paper is accepted is foolish. The scholar's response is that sensor-grounded admission is functionally already a second paper - 18 pages, mechanized Lean, adversarial review clean - and choosing not to ship it after the parent is choosing to leave a publishable result on the floor. Ship-one-and-stop is a defensible strategy if and only if the second paper requires substantial new work. It does not. The marginal cost of shipping sensor-grounded in the same cycle as the parent is conference-template conversion plus anonymization - a few days. Refusing that is not pragmatism, it is leaving value uncollected.

**Pragmatist: "Solo author, five threads, you'll burn out."** Correct that five simultaneous threads is unsustainable. Incorrect that the program requires five simultaneous threads. The staggered schedule has at most two active at any time; the other three are in co-author negotiation, behind a Lean gate, or in research phase. The scholar's program is sequential with overlap, not parallel.

**Substrate-builder: "Build V2-V8 first, then publish on a stronger substrate."** The substrate-builder is solving a problem the field does not have. Top-conference papers in security do not require production-grade federated kernels with threshold cosigning and issuer rotation; they require a clear contribution, sound formal model, and one or two worked examples. The parent paper has all three. Delaying publication to ship V7 FROST/ROAST is the engineer's instinct to fix the imperfect floor before opening the showroom. It is the wrong instinct for an academic program. Publish first, harden later, in response to specific reviewer asks if any.

**Substrate-builder: "Without production deployment, the framework is unfalsifiable theater."** Falsifiability comes from the Lean proofs, the receipt schema, and the worked Compound 289 retrospective. The framework is already falsifiable by anyone who reads the spec and points at a counterexample. Production deployment is not the falsifiability mechanism; it is the adoption mechanism, and adoption follows citation, not the other way around.

**Pragmatist: "What if the parent gets rejected?"** Then the scholar's program absorbs a six-month delay and resubmits. The program does not depend on parent acceptance at the first venue; it depends on parent acceptance somewhere in 12 months. Three top venues plus IEEE S&P plus the option of arXiv-permanent-with-workshop-track makes this a near-certainty.
