\label{sec:limits}

The remaining limits are:

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=2pt,leftmargin=1.2em]
  \item \textbf{Declarative scope only.} A Chio polity has final authority over admission to its own receipt log; it cannot tax, conscript, expel a person, or compel an external court, and Montevideo territoriality holds only in the event-space sense.
  \item \textbf{Bounded paper model and inventory.} The four paper-model theorems encode treaty predicate intersection, ladder-floor stability, and amendment as backward refinement; they do not verify the whole Rust implementation. Curated inventory entries map proof-tree declarations to protocol properties and Rust symbols; coverage rests on inventory links, not raw declaration counts.
  \item \textbf{Anchor lane unevenness.} Rekor SET verification is implemented; Rekor Merkle inclusion-proof verification is follow-up code; the Bitcoin-via-OTS lane is verify-real for parsing and commitment linkage and advisory for load-bearing public-witness policy; the Solana memo lane checks root and checkpoint matching, not chain finality.
  \item \textbf{Benchmark gaps.} Dispatch, treaty intersection, selective disclosure, and replay corpus have script-backed numbers; receipt signing, verification, and anchor inclusion are withheld until dedicated harnesses exist.
  \item \textbf{TEE and federation surfaces.} The MAA TEE verifier is label-only; TDX, SEV-SNP, and Nitro lanes are implemented; the federation crate carries legacy stub surfaces alongside the strict Chio path. Receipt cosigning gives offline auditability but not online kernel attestation, so a TEE-rooted kernel is a strict strengthening complementary to cosigning.
  \item \textbf{Conflict semantics and amendment expressiveness.} A polity can refuse admission, split logs, or deny treaty entry; it cannot seize another polity's receipts or treasury unless those assets were already inside its predicates. Backward refinement preserves admitted history; emergency powers, debt restructuring, amnesty, expulsion, and retroactive statutes require explicit crisis artifacts that break refinement.
  \item \textbf{Legal scope.} Corporate form, data protection, consumer protection, sanctions, insolvency, and venue are institutional questions a Chio polity produces evidence for, not questions it resolves.
  \item \textbf{No end-user surface.} The paper defines the substrate and evidence path; it does not deliver a constitutional interface, dispute console, treaty negotiation tool, or regulator portal.
  \item \textbf{Key custody and partition reconciliation.} Per-kernel Ed25519 signing assumes a key-management story (HSM, KMS, TEE-bound key handles) the paper does not specify; rotation, revocation, and compromise recovery for fleets of thousands of kernels are outside the construction. Partition-contingency mode is named in the ladder, but the reconnect protocol (how forked polities exchange reconciliation evidence and resume admission) is not specified; an unsolvable merge collapses partition-contingency to a one-way door.
  \item \textbf{Portability, interop, and schema evolution.} Receipt schemas are Chio-specific (e.g., \codepath{chio.bilateral-cosign-invocation.v1}); a cross-implementation profile is the prerequisite for any second receiver to admit a Chio receipt without trusting the Chio implementation. Kernels at different schema versions currently deny on \codepath{unsupported_treaty_scope_schema} without distinguishing schema mismatch from constitutional denial; a versioned-predicate profile and a distinct \codepath{schema_mismatch} denial code are required for multi-tenant deployment.
  \item \textbf{Regulator accreditation gap.} No current attestation framework (SOC~2, ISO~27001, PCAOB, sectoral) cites Lean-attested receipt evidence; until one maps receipts to existing control language, Chio is an evidence producer with no accredited consumer.
  \item \textbf{Override authority and observability.} Fail-closed admission produces a denial receipt rather than a fallback path; the paper does not define which capability authorizes admit-under-protest, what crisis artifact it produces, or how the override is audited. The receipt log is the audit channel; live observability (metrics, traces, denial-rate alerts) requires a non-leaking telemetry path the substrate does not specify.
  \item \textbf{Cryptographic-suite migration and PQC trajectory.} The substrate pins Ed25519, BBS, and SHA-256; cross-polity rotation lacks a defined protocol for re-anchoring existing receipt corpora, and the BBS lane will need re-evaluation under post-quantum mandates. The likely substitution is salted-hash formats (SD-JWT VC, ISO mdoc) signed under PQC algorithms, the path ETSI TR~119~476-1 and the EUDI ARF take, not a transparent-SNARK upgrade of BBS.
\end{itemize}
