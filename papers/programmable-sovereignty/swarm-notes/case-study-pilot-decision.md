Three pilots emerged from the swarm:

1. **Healthcare AI agent compliance** (round-2 tactical agent's pick): HIPAA + EU AI Act Annex III high-risk clinical decision support; concrete pilot path with academic medical center + LLM clinical scribe vendor; Q3 2026 first deployment.

3. **AI cross-lab red-team attestation** (round-3 AI safety agent's pick): UK AISI accepting US AISIC evaluation without forcing methodology disclosure; bilateral DSSE between frontier labs and government safety institutes.

## Decision

**Pick (3) AI cross-lab red-team attestation as the v2 paper's case study; defer (1) and (2) to follow-up case-study papers.**

Reasons:

- **Strongest match to v2 substantive lift** (paper 1 in iter-8 forward-looker pipeline: "Federated Receipt Admission Between Two Independently-Operated Lean-Attested Kernels", NDSS 2027 target). Two real frontier safety institutes co-signing a bilateral DSSE is exactly the "live two-kernel federation across a real network boundary" the senior-PC blocker requires.
- **Highest-leverage partner already identified**: UK AISI per round-3 AI safety agent. State-funded, mandate explicitly includes frontier deployment evaluation, Inspect framework needs runtime evidence surface; the relationship is institutional and reproducible.
- **Closes the §7 AI safety paragraph's "regulator-co-signing key" forward-pointer** in §10 conclusion. Currently §10 says "willing co-signing counterparty"; UK AISI is the most concrete instantiation.
- **Does NOT require waiting on regulatory accreditation gap** (§9 limitation). AISI is a deployment evaluator, not a sectoral SRO; its co-signing is a research-grade partnership rather than a compliance attestation.
- **(1) and (2) remain in the follow-up pipeline**: (1) is the right Q3 2026 enterprise-revenue play but does not need to land in v2 to make the paper credible; (2) is the most-novel reframing but is also the most-controversial cosigner sequence and should follow v2 publication, not gate it.
