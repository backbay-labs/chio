\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:evaluation}

The empirical claim is bounded to the substrate's verifier-side discipline on a sensor-state receipt. Absent measurements are marked rather than estimated.

\paragraph{Verifier-side rejection on attestation mutation.}
The validator at \codepath{require\_sensor\_state\_evidence} hash-binds six evidence rows: \codepath{providerCount}, \codepath{activeProviderCount}, \codepath{healthyProviderCount}, \codepath{degradedProviderCount}, \codepath{providerIds}, \codepath{sensorStateHash}. Each must match a re-hash over the receipt's \codepath{sensor\_state} field under a fail-closed rule-id assertion. A companion check, \codepath{require\_provider\_degradation\_consistency}, rejects any record whose flags and degradation-reason list contradict the \codepath{degraded} flag.

The load-bearing measurement is a verifier-side mutation test, \codepath{endpoint\_sensor\_state\_receipt\_binds\_provider\_health}. It signs a two-provider receipt (healthy \codepath{agentApi}; degraded \codepath{macos.endpoint\_security} with \codepath{dropped\_event\_count=2}, \codepath{deadline\_miss\_count=1}, missing full-disk access), then perturbs it one mutation at a time and asserts \codepath{validate()} returns \codepath{Err}. Mutations cover tampered \codepath{providerCount}, \codepath{providerIds}, \codepath{activeProviderCount}, and \codepath{sensorStateHash} rows; a relabeled provider identifier; an inconsistent \codepath{degraded} flag; missing or future-dated \codepath{last\_seen} on an active provider; and a cleared or blank degradation-reason list. Every mutation is denied with a substring-matched error. \codepath{healthyProviderCount} and \codepath{degradedProviderCount} are exercised transitively, since the consistency check rejects flag-field perturbations before those rows are reached; a coordinated mutation that bypasses the consistency check to perturb them directly is named below as withheld.

\paragraph{Receipt-emitter survey.}
The macOS host snapshot is built by \codepath{endpoint\_sensor\_state\_from\_macos\_host} in \codepath{api\_server}. Six emitter sites consume it, covering response-execution receipts and the sensor-state receipt itself. Thirteen further sites use a placeholder, \codepath{EndpointSensorState::single\_active\_agent("agent-api")}: one record, healthy, no degradation. The placeholder is a structural constant; the validator's hash-bound rows are coherent on it by construction. The empirical claim covers the strict subset of receipts emitted at the six host-snapshot sites.

\paragraph{Verification cost.}
The validator re-hashes six evidence rows and the canonical-JSON encoding of \codepath{sensor\_state} via \codepath{endpoint\_sensor\_state\_content\_hash}. The work is O(provider count) under SHA-256. No latency harness exists in the substrate's bench surface; a Criterion bench over the sign and validate paths is withheld. The canonical-JSON byte size is bounded by provider count: roughly 200--400 bytes for the placeholder, 600--1200 bytes for a three-provider host snapshot. Receipt-ledger storage grows linearly in provider count.

\paragraph{What is not measured.}
The macOS \codepath{EndpointSecurity} system extension shipped with the substrate sources events from an in-memory recorder rather than calling \codepath{es\_new\_client} or \codepath{es\_subscribe}. The host-snapshot path's ES provider record is therefore real in shape but synthetic in content: runtime flags, install state, drop and miss counts, and full-disk-access signal are recorder-supplied, not derived from a live ES client. The sensor input layer the empirical claim covers is the network-extension filter (real on the egress reload path), the package-manager hooks, and the tool-preflight surface; ES-derived telemetry is out of scope.

A deployment-rate partition-contingency number is not reported: aggregating host-snapshot and placeholder populations would conflate a host posture with a structural constant. A false-attestation rate is not reported (Section~\ref{sec:limits}): no out-of-band sensor-coverage auditor is part of the substrate. The Criterion latency bench, and a direct mutation across \codepath{healthyProviderCount} and \codepath{degradedProviderCount} on an attestation that bypasses the consistency check, are withheld; the structural placement of both is well-defined.
